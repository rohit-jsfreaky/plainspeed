The article is already fast: it scores 95 on phones and 91 on desktop, and its main content appears in 1.3 seconds on a phone. Two things hold it back on phones. The layout jumps once while loading (a shift of 0.11, just over Google's 0.1 limit), and the page takes 3.9 seconds to fill in. Fixing the jump and the slow first response from the server are the changes worth making first.

What to fix first

1. Things move around while the page loads. Content jumping makes people tap the wrong thing. Reserving space for images, ads and embeds stops it.

2. The server is slow to start answering. Nothing can appear until the page's first byte arrives. Faster hosting, caching or a CDN brings everything forward.

3. Returning visitors download files again. Longer cache lifetimes let repeat visits load from the visitor's own device.
